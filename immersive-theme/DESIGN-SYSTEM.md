## Design Direction: Premium Shamanic

**Reference:** Mindvalley.com - Professional, polished, premium feel
**Goal:** Apply their layout principles to shamanic/healing theme

## Typography System

| Element | Font | Size Desktop | Size Mobile | Weight |
|---------|------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| Hero Title | Cinzel | 72-96px | 40-48px | 600 |
| Section Title | Cinzel | 56-72px | 32-40px | 600 |
| Subtitle | Cinzel | 28-36px | 22-28px | 400 |
| Body Large | Lato | 18-20px | 16-18px | 400 |
| Body | Lato | 16px | 15px | 400 |
| Caption | Lato | 13-14px | 12px | 400 |
| Eyebrow | Cinzel | 11-12px | 10px | 600 |

**Letter spacing:** Headings -0.02em, Body 0, Eyebrow 4-6px uppercase

## Layout Principles

1. **Full-viewport heroes** - 100vh, image-dominant
2. **Generous padding** - 120-160px section padding desktop
3. **Max-width containers** - 1200-1400px content max
4. **Asymmetrical layouts** - Not always centered, use visual weight
5. **Card-based content** - Image cards for teachings/events/testimonials
6. **Minimal decorations** - No heavy patterns, let images speak

## Animation Principles

- Scroll-triggered only (Intersection Observer)
- Subtle: opacity + translateY
- Duration: 600-800ms
- Easing: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)
- No bouncing, no spinning, no distracting motion

## Section Structure (Rebuild Order)

1. Hero - Full viewport, dramatic image, massive headline
2. Social Proof - Press logos bar
3. About - Split layout with large photo
4. Teachings - 3 large image cards
5. Events - Featured card + side cards
6. Testimonials - Large quote cards with photos
7. Gallery - Full-width image grid
8. Video - Video cards with thumbnails
9. Transformation - Timeline on dark background
10. Final CTA - Dark section, simple, powerful
11. Footer - Minimal, dark
